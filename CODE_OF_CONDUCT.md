# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Bela Learning to be a place where parents, teachers and developers can
contribute without being harassed — and a piece of software that treats children with
respect. We pledge to make participation a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that builds this project:

- being kind and patient, especially with beginners,
- giving feedback about the work, not the person,
- accepting that a five-year-old's attention span is a design constraint,
- crediting other people's lessons, art and ideas,
- saying "I don't know" when you don't.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention,
- insulting, demeaning or discriminatory comments,
- publishing someone's private information without permission,
- adding tracking, advertising or data collection to the app,
- adding content that frightens, shames or pressures children.

## Scope

This applies in the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere someone
represents the project.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers. Reports are handled privately and
fairly. Maintainers may remove comments, close contributions, or ban participants when
needed.
